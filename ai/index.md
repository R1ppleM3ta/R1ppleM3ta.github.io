## What this site is
The Consequence Record is an independent research blog on how leaders decide, delay, and carry the cost of delay.

## What it covers
Decision-making in leadership teams: delayed decisions, succession, partner deadlock, board decision paralysis, and the cost of delay. See the [topics hub](/topics/).

## Who edits it
Ben Hosking, founder of Ripple Meta, a Brisbane leadership practice. Ripple Meta (https://ripplemeta.com) is Ben's company.

## Who publishes it
Ben Hosking. The site is separate from ripplemeta.com.

## How articles are sourced
Outside studies are listed in the [research index](/research-index/) with author, year, source and link. Ripple Meta's own work is cited here and is always labelled as such. Rules are in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Terms that originate here
Authority deferral is a term from Ben Hosking's Sovereign Leadership work at Ripple Meta. See the [glossary entry](/glossary/authority-deferral/).
