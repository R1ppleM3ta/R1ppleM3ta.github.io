The Consequence Record is an independent research blog on how leaders decide, delay, and carry the cost of delay. It collects and explains research on decision-making in leadership teams, with a focus on authority deferral: the point where a leader holds the authority and the clarity to act, and delays or softens the decision to avoid cost. Ripple Meta's own work is cited here and is always labelled as such.
