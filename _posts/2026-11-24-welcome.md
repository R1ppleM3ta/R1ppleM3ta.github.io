The Consequence Record is a research blog on how leaders decide, delay, and carry the cost of delay.

It is edited by Ben Hosking, founder of Ripple Meta, a Brisbane leadership practice. Ripple Meta's own work is cited here and is always labelled as such.

Expect short posts that start from one question a leader or a board is facing, name the research that bears on it, and say plainly what is and is not known. Outside studies are listed in the [research index](/research-index/). The rules for sourcing and corrections are in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
