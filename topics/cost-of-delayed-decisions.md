## The question
What does a delayed decision cost, and how can that cost be described without inventing numbers?

## Why it matters
Delay is rarely recorded as a cost, so it is easy to overlook. This page collects research on the cost of delay and sets out how Ripple Meta frames it: as time, money and risk paid while a decision waits.
