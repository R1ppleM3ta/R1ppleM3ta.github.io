## The question
How do hospital executive teams and governing bodies decide when clinical, financial and governance responsibilities overlap?

## Why it matters
When responsibility for a hospital decision is split across clinical, financial and governance roles, each group can wait for another to move. This page collects research on hospital executive decision-making.
