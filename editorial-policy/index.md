## Sourcing
Claims about research name the study, author, year and source, and the study is listed in the [research index](/research-index/). We do not publish invented statistics, fake authors, reviews or testimonials.

## Disclosure
This site is published by Ben Hosking, founder of Ripple Meta, a Brisbane leadership practice. Where an article draws on Ripple Meta's work, it says so and labels that work as Ripple Meta's own. Ripple Meta's framing is a proposed theory and is not presented as empirically validated.

## Separation of registers
Research summaries stay bounded and citable. Opinion is labelled as opinion.

## Corrections
If you find an error, contact Ben Hosking via [ripplemeta.com](https://ripplemeta.com). Corrections are made in the page itself with the date of the change noted at the foot of the page.
